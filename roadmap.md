# Roadmap

## What this is
A study task list for people learning a language. Each task is tagged with a skill (Listening, Speaking, Reading, or Writing) so the learner can see which skill they have been avoiding.

## What Done means
A stranger opens the live site, creates an account with an email and password, adds a few study tasks each tagged Listening, Speaking, Reading, or Writing, checks off the ones they finish, and sees how many finished tasks they have per skill, with the lowest skill marked as the one they are avoiding. When they come back later and log in, their tasks and counts are still there, and no one else can see them.

## Slices
1. Sign up and log in | done-criteria: (a) In a private browser window on the live site, click Sign up, enter a new email and a password, submit; the page shows "Signed in as" followed by that email. (b) Click Log out; the email disappears and a Log in button shows. (c) Log in with the same email and a wrong password; an error message appears and you stay logged out. (d) Log in correctly, close the browser, reopen the live site; you are still signed in. | status: ACTIVE
2. Tasks tagged by skill | done-criteria: (a) Signed in, type "Watch one news clip", choose Listening, click Add; the task appears in the list labeled Listening. (b) Tick its checkbox, log out, log back in; the task is still there and still ticked. (c) Log in as a second account; the first account's task does not appear. | status: pending
3. Skill balance | done-criteria: (a) On an account with finished tasks of 3 Reading, 2 Listening, 1 Speaking, 0 Writing, the page shows exactly those four numbers and Writing is marked "Avoiding". (b) Finish one Writing task and reload; Writing shows 1, and both Speaking and Writing are marked "Avoiding" because they tie for lowest. (c) On a brand-new account, all four skills show 0 with the message "No finished tasks yet", not a blank area or an error. | status: pending
